what's the science? (tap)

the idea ("stochastic resonance" / moderate brain arousal): some ADHD brains run under-stimulated, and a steady background noise adds just enough input to sharpen focus. too little does nothing, too much distracts — the sweet spot is different for everyone.

real talk: a 2024 meta-analysis found a small benefit for ADHD folks with white/pink noise (and a slight downside for non-ADHD people). brown noise is the internet favourite and the most comfy, but has the least direct research. some people do best in silence — the finder will tell u.

how it's made: generated live so it never loops · independent left/right channels · no volume swells · rumble below 35 Hz and harsh hiss above 6 kHz filtered out of pink + brown (white stays raw, like in the studies) · all 3 colours matched in loudness.
